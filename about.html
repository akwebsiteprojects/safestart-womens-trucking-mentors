About SafeStart

Starting over is not a setback. It's a strength.

SafeStart exists so women, especially those 40 and over, can enter the trucking industry with confidence, safety and belief in themselves.

Our mission

Skills, confidence and opportunity.

To empower women with the skills, confidence and opportunities needed to build stable, successful careers in trucking and equipment operation.

Our vision

A future with more women behind the wheel.

A future where women, especially those 40+, enter the trucking industry with confidence, safety and unwavering belief in themselves.

Meet the founder

Sandra Williams, Founder & Mentor

At 42, Sandra knew nothing about trucking. She was broke, unemployed and living in a shelter.

One person believed in her, and that belief changed everything. She learned, persevered and built a stable, independent life through trucking.

Today, at 63, she is still proudly operating. She created SafeStart to give other women the same opportunity she fought for.

“One person believed in me. Now I want to believe in you.”

Why SafeStart works

Because women deserve a safe start.

Many women grow up hearing that trucking is “only for men.” SafeStart is here to break that myth.

We offer a calm, supportive environment where it's safe to ask questions, make mistakes and build confidence. No intimidation, no pressure, no fear.

You deserve

  • Real support
  • Belief in your potential

  • A mentor who understands your journey
