Welcome to SafeStart

A safe place to begin again.

SafeStart is a calm, supportive, safety-first mentorship program for women ready to rebuild, restart or rise into a new chapter. You don't have to know anything about trucks to take the first step.

✓ Respect for your pace

Every woman grows at her own speed. No pressure to learn like anyone else.

✓ Judgment-free conversations

Bring your questions, goals and uncertainties. It's safe to ask and safe to make mistakes.

✓ Safety before everything

Responsible choices and qualified training matter, on day one and every day after.

✓ Belief in your potential

One encouraging voice can make a difference. We meet you where you are.

What we offer

Meet the founder

From starting over to moving forward.

At 42, Sandra Williams was broke, unemployed and living in a shelter. She knew nothing about trucks or trucking. Then one person believed in her.

Today, at 63, she is still proudly operating. SafeStart is her way of passing that belief forward.

“If I could rebuild my life at 42, so can you.”Sandra Williams, Founder & Mentor
